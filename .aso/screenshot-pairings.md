## Confirmed benefits → recommended pairings

1. **PRICE OUT TRADES FAST** → `04-trade-active.png` (Great)  
   Why: Directly shows both sides priced with the imbalance — the #1 reason to download.

2. **FIND THE PERFECT FILLER** → `02-find-trade-filler.png` (Great)  
   Why: Feature is unique and the list of exact/near matches sells it instantly.

3. **SCAN CARDS INSTANTLY** → **MISSING**  
   Need: Camera scan UI with a real card in frame (guide overlay / match result if available). Clean status bar.

4. **TRACK YOUR BINDER VALUE** → `03-binder.png` (Usable)  
   Why: Binder value + priced card row.  
   💡 Better if: 5–8 cards in the list, binder value looks substantial ($50+ looks more impressive than $1.20).

5. **SAVE YOUR TRADE HISTORY** → **MISSING**  
   Need: Trade history list with several past trades and values visible.

## Retake guidance

### Scan (required for benefit 3)
- Open the card scanner (from Trade or Binder “Scan a card to add”).
- Hold/frame a real Riftbound card so art + name are readable.
- Prefer a state that shows recognition or a clear scan guide overlay (not a blank/black camera).
- Status bar: full signal, full battery, clean time (e.g. 9:41).

### Trade history (required for benefit 5)
- Open Trade → History.
- Have at least 3–5 past trades listed with card counts and dollar amounts.
- Avoid empty “No history yet” state.

### Optional binder upgrade (benefit 4)
- Add more cards so the list fills the screen; bump binder value to something that reads well at thumbnail size.
