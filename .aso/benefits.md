# RiftTrades — ASO Benefits

- **App name:** RiftTrades
- **Bundle ID:** (Flutter iOS — see apps/mobile)
- **Target audience:** Riftbound players at locals and larger events who want to price trades quickly without a notes app or juggling multiple pages.
- **Niche:** Riftbound (Riot League of Legends TCG) companion — prices, trade balancing, binder, lending, scan.
- **#1 download reason:** Easily price out trades in one place; also save trade histories.

## Confirmed benefits (App Store order)

1. **PRICE OUT TRADES FAST** — Live both-sides totals and the dollar gap in one screen.
2. **FIND THE PERFECT FILLER** — Closest-match cards to balance the trade.
3. **SCAN CARDS INSTANTLY** — Add cards from camera instead of typing.
4. **TRACK YOUR BINDER VALUE** — Collection worth at a glance with market + low prices.
5. **SAVE YOUR TRADE HISTORY** — Revisit past trades after the event.
